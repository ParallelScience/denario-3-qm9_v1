**Title**: Decoupling Non-Additive Energetics via Graph-Latent Residual Decomposition

**Description**: We will isolate non-additive electronic contributions to internal energy (`u0`) by training a baseline additive model based on RDKit-derived bond-type and functional-group counts, avoiding problematic per-atom normalization. We will then employ a Graph Neural Network (GNN) to predict the residuals of this additive baseline, utilizing the network's latent space to capture long-range topological features—such as extended π-conjugation pathways and ring-oxygen configurations—that linear models inherently miss. By applying Integrated Gradients to the GNN, we will map these non-additive residuals to specific molecular subgraphs, identifying which motifs exert the greatest influence on energetic stability. Finally, we will correlate these graph-derived "non-additivity scores" with intensive properties like `gap` and `mu` to quantify how structural electronic effects deviate from classical group-contribution theory. This approach rigorously separates the extensive scaling of the QM9 dataset from the underlying non-linear electronic physics without requiring 3D coordinate generation.
